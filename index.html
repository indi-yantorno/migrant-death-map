\ \ \ \ \ Where recorded deaths cluster, El Paso Sector\ \ \ \
\
\

Recorded deaths cluster in one corridor around El Paso and Do\'f1a Ana County

\

Where along the El Paso Sector are recorded migrant deaths concentrated, and where could prevention and rescue resources have the greatest impact?

\
\
\ \
\
\ \ Map of recorded deaths in the El Paso Sector. 55% of mapped cases fall in a corridor of roughly 21 by 26 miles around El Paso and Sunland Park.\ 78% are in Do\'f1a Ana and El Paso counties. 56% occurred from June to August, and environmental exposure is the leading cause.\ \
\ \
\

About the data. El Paso Sector Migrant Deaths dataset (UNM Office of the Medical Investigator and partner sources). Of 709 recorded cases from 2008 to 2024, 662 are shown.

\

Limits. Dots show where remains were recovered or estimated, not where a journey began. Cases with a location accuracy worse than 2 miles (or no location) are left out, along with 5 cases north of the map frame. Border and state lines are approximate. Only 65 mapped cases fall before 2019, so comparisons with later years should be read with care. Recorded deaths only; remains never found are not counted.
